# Features, release 0.4.0 (and the picker fix in 0.3.1)
Status: Ready

## Problem
Players cannot see their best eleven or how deep each position is without counting by hand. The original's player comparison shows two lists of numbers and no picture of the difference. The player profile lists 31 attributes in alphabetical order, so a player hunts for the mental or physical ones. The print buttons do nothing in our build when Print To is Printer. On a 1080p or 4K screen the game draws at 1280x800 and scales up, so big screens get bigger pixels, not more space. And "Pick best team", released in 0.3.0, benches natural players: on Max's Yeovil save it put Warner (a natural DR) on the bench and O'Brien at DMC with a fit of 10.

## Solution
Release 0.3.1 fixes the Assistant pick. Release 0.4.0 adds a Team views screen with Best XI, Squad depth and Compare players tabs, a polygon on the original's comparison screen, attribute groups on the player profile, prints as PDF files and an HD mode at 1920x1080. No feature changes a match result or a game decision, except the team that the player or the assistant picks. Every new option is a line in the settings file.

## User stories
1. As a player, I want "Pick best team" to put natural players in their positions, so that I do not have to fix the assistant's team by hand.
2. As a player with a weak squad, I want the assistant to play a player out of position only when nobody fits the slot.
3. As a player, I want the assistant to consider reserve-team players who are on my squad screen, as I can.
4. As a player, I want to see my best eleven on a pitch in my tactic's formation.
5. As a player, I want to try another formation and see who would play in it, without a change to my team.
6. As a player, I want to see the top three players for each position, so that I know where my squad is thin.
7. As a player with an injured first choice, I want best XI to show who plays on Saturday, and the depth table to still list the injured player in grey.
8. As a player scouting the next opponent, I want the same views for any other club's squad.
9. As a player, I want to compare two players with a polygon of their strengths, as in later Football Manager games.
10. As a player comparing two goalkeepers, I want goalkeeper axes, not outfield ones.
11. As a player who knows the original, I want to choose the two players with the original's "Set as 1st/2nd player in comparison?" prompts.
12. As a player, I want the profile's attributes grouped into Technical, Mental and Physical, with headings.
13. As a player looking at a goalkeeper, I want a Goalkeeping group first and no outfield-only attributes, as in later games.
14. As a player who prefers the original's look, I want to switch the groups off.
15. As a player, I want the print buttons to make a PDF of the squad list or a table, so that I can keep or share it.
16. As a player who uses the original's text printouts, I want the File choice to work as before.
17. As a player with a 1080p or 4K screen, I want an HD mode that uses the space.
18. As a player who asked for a feature on Reddit, I want a credit in the release post when it ships.

## Decisions
- **Same game (Q152, 2026-10-16):** a feature must not change the game's decisions while the player makes the same clicks. A change of memory layout counts like opening a screen. Research (slices 3 and 7): the original's results depend on where its memory blocks sit; opening any screen, the original's own included, changes later days, and classic and widescreen saves differ for the same reason.
- **Classic headings (Q153):** classic mode uses 13 rows of 17 px so that all group headings show (replaces the fallback of Q125).
- **Covered names (Q150):** a clickable name shows no underline while a box or popup covers it.
- **Best XI layout (Q154, Q155):** the pitch is wide enough that every name box sits inside it. The right half of the Best XI tab lists the subs and the reserves with their ratings for the chosen formation.
- **Goal box (Q151):** the goal box stays where the original puts it, over the third scorer row.
Settled in the interview of 2026-10-15 (Q100 to Q155). Research facts are marked as such.

- **Scope and split (Q100):** 0.4.0 holds #22, #32, #33 and #43. If HD mode needs more time than the rest, 0.4.0 ships without it and HD follows as 0.4.1. The picker fix ships first as 0.3.1 (Q133).
- **Options (Q101):** `display = hd`; `attribute_groups = on` (default on); `squad_views = on` (default on; Team views screen and the polygon); the Print To game option keeps its place in the game's options (see print below). HD mode is opt-in; widescreen stays the default (Q113).
- **Credits:** u/gundenaf asked for the polygon analyser, HD mode and attribute groups (`docs/requests.md`).

### Assistant pick (0.3.1)
- **Score (Q138, Q139; replace Q135 and Q136):** the average of the key shown attributes for the slot's position, times positional fit (the game's position rating, 1 to 20) divided by 20. Current ability is not used. Reason: on Max's Yeovil save, current ability times fit put Weale in goal over D. Williams (Agility 20, Bravery 20, Determination 20, current ability 62 against 70) and G. Williams at AMC over Moukoko (Technique 17, Decisions 20). Agility, Jumping, Bravery, Determination, Technique and Pace are fixed values that current ability does not feed. The key attributes:
  - GK: Handling, Reflexes, Agility, Positioning, Jumping
  - DC: Tackling, Marking, Heading, Positioning, Strength, Jumping
  - DL, DR: Tackling, Marking, Pace, Positioning, Crossing
  - DMC: Tackling, Positioning, Passing, Teamwork, Work Rate
  - MC: Passing, Decisions, Teamwork, Work Rate, Technique
  - ML, MR: Crossing, Dribbling, Pace, Passing, Technique
  - AMC (and AML, AMR): Passing, Creativity, Decisions, Technique, Flair
  - FC: Finishing, Off The Ball, Anticipation, Technique, Pace (Q140; Heading alone benched Bergkamp behind Aliadière)
  The picker slice may adjust a list only with Max's yes on the Yeovil table.
- **Assignment (Q130):** the eleven is the assignment with the highest total score over all slots, found exactly (for example the Hungarian method over 11 slots and up to about 50 players). The subs come from the players left over. The 0.3.0 method filled one slot at a time and its swap pass never brought in a bench player.
- **Out of position (Q131, Q142):** a player with a fit below 10 goes into a slot only when no free player has a fit of 10 or more for it. In Pick most match-fit team and Pick under-21 team, this rule comes before the group: a player outside the group with a fit of 10 or more takes the slot before a group player with a fit below 10.
- **Reserves (Q134):** reserve-team players on the squad screen are candidates.
- **Same rule for best XI (Q132):** one pick function computes the team without writing it. "Pick best team" writes its result; best XI only shows it.
- Research facts (2026-10-15): the picker reads current ability at player data +0x05, proven from the game's attribute getter (1115 of 1115 values match). The game's position text shows "AM" only when the AM and M ratings are both 15 or more, so G. Williams ("F RC") has AM 20. Each shown attribute is computed from a hidden per-attribute value plus current ability, capped at 20 (champman0102.co.uk forum, topic 4251).

### Team views screen (#22)
- **Where (Q102):** one new screen with three tabs, Best XI, Squad depth and Compare players, opened by a "Team views" item in the Assistant drop-down on the squad screen. Max's mockup of 2026-10-15 shows the layout: the club bar, the tabs and a Formation drop-down, a pitch on the left and the depth table on the right.
- **Other clubs (Q108):** the screen also opens from any other club's squad screen.
- **View only (Q104):** best XI and squad depth never change the team.
- **Formation (Q103, Q128):** best XI opens on the formation of the player's current tactic. The Formation drop-down lists the same tactics as the game's own Tactics drop-down. Research needed: the tactic file format.
- **Best XI (Q105, Q106):** the eleven from the pick function, each with his position rating as the number, coloured by the attribute colour scale. Injured and suspended players are left out. Current ability is never shown.
- **Squad depth (Q106, Q107):** one row for each position of the chosen formation (DL and DR apart), the top three players in each row by the pick score. A player can appear in more than one row. Injured and suspended players are listed in grey.
- **New screen (my decision):** the first build task tests whether the runtime can push a new screen onto the screen stack. If it cannot, the views open in place of the comparison screen. Result (2026-10-15): the push works. The screen's build and input are host functions (`src/recomp_rt/win/views.c`); Back, Next and the side bar work. Frame facts: `docs/research/2026-10-15-team-views-frame-inventory.md`.
- **Research fact (2026-10-15):** the game's results depend on the layout of its heap. One more 11-byte block made only with an option on gave a different save after one day. An option must make the same heap blocks on and off. The original's own screens change later days too (a visit of the league table does), so the same-save check keeps the steps equal and leaves the new screen shut. Research fact: the original has no diagonal line or polygon fill and no pitch picture, so the pitch and the polygon need new runtime draw code.

### Comparison and polygon (#22)
- **The original's comparison (Q116):** the original has "Compare two chosen players" in the side bar, and the prompts "Set as 1st/2nd player in comparison?" choose the players. That screen gets the new layout. No new Compare button (replaces Q109). The Compare tab of the Team views screen opens the same screen, with two drop-downs that pick from the squad.
- **Layout (Q114, Q115):** variant B of the prototype: the first player's attributes on the left, the polygon in the middle, the second player's attributes on the right. Attributes in their groups, numbers in attribute colours, the higher value of each pair marked. The two shapes overlap, one blue and one orange, with a legend.
- **Axes (Q110, Q117):** eight axes, each the average of shown attributes:
  - Defending: Tackling, Marking, Positioning
  - Physical: Strength, Stamina, Agility, Balance
  - Speed: Pace, Acceleration
  - Vision: Passing, Creativity, Flair
  - Attacking: Finishing, Off The Ball, Long Shots
  - Technique: Technique, Dribbling, Crossing
  - Aerial: Heading, Jumping
  - Mental: Decisions, Anticipation, Work Rate, Teamwork, Determination
  Two goalkeepers get a goalkeeper set of axes, which the slice proposes from the shown attributes and Max approves on a screenshot.
- **Hidden values (Q111):** values that the original does not show never feed the polygon, the groups or the comparison. Research fact: the profile shows 31 attributes. Current ability, potential ability and the others in the player data (for example Consistency, Important Matches, Injury Proneness, Natural Fitness) are not among them. Max holds that only current and potential ability are hidden; if a screen shows any of the others, Q111 and the groups take it in.

### Attribute groups (#43)
- **Layout (Q122, Q124):** each of the profile's three columns holds one group with a heading, alphabetical inside the group:
  - Column 1: Technical (Crossing, Dribbling, Finishing, Heading, Long Shots, Marking, Passing, Set Pieces, Tackling, Technique)
  - Column 2: Mental (Aggression, Anticipation, Bravery, Creativity, Decisions, Determination, Flair, Influence, Off The Ball, Positioning, Teamwork, Work Rate)
  - Column 3: Physical (Acceleration, Agility, Balance, Jumping, Pace, Stamina, Strength), then Preferred Foot, Form, Morale, Condition and On Loan
- **Goalkeepers (Q129, replaces Q123):** column 1 is Goalkeeping (Handling, Reflexes, Passing), then a short Technical group (Set Pieces, Technique). Crossing, Dribbling, Finishing, Heading, Long Shots, Marking and Tackling are not shown for goalkeepers. An outfield player shows no Handling or Reflexes. Display only: no value changes.
- **Fit (Q125):** if a heading row does not fit the area in classic mode, the group name goes in the column title.
- **Where (Q126):** the player profile and the comparison only. The squad list's Attributes columns stay as they are.
- Research facts: the original sorts the 31 names with qsort and lays out 36 rows of 3 columns by 12; there is no group data. The attribute colour hook keys on return addresses and survives a new order. 39 rows overflow the 36-row buffer, so the slice needs a bigger table.

### Print to PDF (#33)
- **Research facts:** the original prints from the squad screen and the competition screens only. A printout is plain text lines (squad lists, player stats, fixtures, results, tables, transfers), not a picture. Print To has Printer, File (a .txt through the "Save Printout" dialog) and Both. In our build the Printer path does nothing and shows no error.
- **Printer becomes PDF (Q118, Q119):** the game shows "PDF" in place of "Printer", and it is the default. PDF writes the printout to a `Prints` folder in the save folder, with a name such as "Squad - Arsenal 2001-07-20.pdf", and shows the path in a message. It does not open the file (Q112). File keeps the original's .txt and dialog. Both writes the PDF and the .txt.
- **Content (Q120):** the original's printout text in a fixed-width font with the original's page breaks.
- **New screens (Q121):** best XI, squad depth and the comparison have no print in 0.4.0.

### Custom data sets (Q143, Q144)
- The Database choice on Setup Game also lists each folder in a `Databases` folder in the save folder, by folder name. The player copies the files of a database zip into one folder, for example `Databases/May 2026`. A folder needs the data files that the game loads; a folder without them is not listed.
- Research fact (2026-10-15): the CM 01/02 Update Team publishes about 49 databases (October 2003 to May 2026) on champman0102.co.uk. Their "unpatched" databases install over the plain 3.9.68 data and keep the 2001/02 start. Downloads need a forum login.
- The downloads ship only the official data. The site credits the Update Team and links to their forum.
- The start year stays July 2001 (Q144). A start-year option and a season screen are planned for 0.5.0.

### HD mode (#32)
- **How (research):** widescreen comes from one scale rule in the area and widget create wraps, nine patched limits and about five screens with their own fixes. HD uses the same rule with 1920x1080 numbers. On a 4K screen HD scales by exactly 2x.
- **Text size (Q127):** text and pictures keep their pixel size in 0.4.0, with no font scaling. On a 1080p screen the text is about three quarters of its size in widescreen full screen. Font scaling would be a later issue if players ask.

## Testing
- **Picker check (new):** a script runs "Pick best team" from a saved game and dumps each slot's player, rating and score. It runs on `start.sav` and on a copy of Max's Yeovil save in `/Users/max/Projects/cm0102/test-fixtures` (outside git). Max sees the Yeovil eleven next to his own before the slice merges (Q137).
- **Smoke run** in classic, widescreen and (from slice 9) HD mode after every slice.
- **Same-save check:** `squad_views`, `attribute_groups` and the PDF choice must each give identical saves on and off.
- **Screen checks:** each drawing slice dumps the screen in each mode and shows before and after screenshots of about 800 px.
- **Options:** `ctest -R options` covers each new option line and its default.
- **Print check:** the text of the PDF matches the .txt printout of the same screen.
- **Manual checks by Max:** HD on a 1080p and a 4K screen; the colours and goalkeeper axes on screenshots.
- Test games run in parallel. If a parallel run fails on timing, run it alone before you call it a bug.
